# Gameplay

Welcome to the gameplay workflow documentation page! Here you'll find a concise overview of our interactive digital marketplace game. This guide explains each step of the gameplay process—from. Whether you’re a new player or a developer, this page will help you understand the core mechanics and flow of the game.



## **LLM Consumer Experience: Login and Tutorial**
Users log in by entering their unique BU ID, ensuring secure and personalized access. Once authenticated, the system launches a brief, step-by-step tutorial that guides users through the platform's core features. Checking for understanding at the end.

## **LLM Consumer Experience: Consumer Round**
In each round, players assume the role of a consumer with a limited amount of money and a set time limit. They must decide quickly whether to purchase products that are either warranted for their quality or unwarranted, potentially misleading options, balancing risk and reward within their budget.
<img src="/img/oneround.gif" alt="LLM Consumer Log In" width="748" height="512"/>

## **LLM Consumer Experience: Feedback Stage**
After the purchasing stage, the feedback stage kicks in with a feedback card that reveals whether you were misled by product quality or advertising. It also offers an option to challenge the warrant of a warranted product, followed by a summary of any challenges made. Finally, the consumer points matrix is displayed again before the next round begins.
